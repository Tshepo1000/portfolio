Developer, working across code and the infrastructure underneath it.

I'm currently a developer at Vukela Connect, working across technical infrastructure, client-facing tooling and product development. Before that, I spent close to two years at Njinjicom Pty Ltd, where I built and hosted client websites and ran the billing side of things through WHMCS.
