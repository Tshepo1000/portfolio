About

Developer, working across code and the infrastructure underneath it.

Based in Gauteng, South Africa. I build fast, accessible websites and apps — and look after the servers, security and cloud services that keep them running.

I'm a developer with a background that spans Java programming, web development, cybersecurity and cloud computing. I like problems that sit at the intersection of code and infrastructure — building a site is only half the job; keeping it fast, secure and online is the other half.

Alongside client-facing development, I've managed hosting environments, DNS and SSL, and automated billing systems. That hands-on side of the work shapes how I build: with reliability and maintainability in mind from the start, not bolted on afterwards.

I'm currently a developer at Vukela Connect, working across technical infrastructure, client-facing tooling and product development. Before that, I spent close to two years at Njinjicom Pty Ltd, where I built and hosted client websites and ran the billing side of things through WHMCS.
